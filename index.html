Our Story

Welcome to Annamalai Residency

❖ ❖ ❖

Conveniently located on Chennai Bypass Road near ES Engineering College, we have been a trusted halt for travellers and a favourite dining spot for families of Viluppuram.

Whether you are passing through on the bypass, arriving at Villupuram Junction just 1.9 km away, or looking for a wholesome family meal, Hotel Annamalai Residency offers clean, comfortable accommodation and a multi-cuisine restaurant under one roof.

Our kitchen serves time-honoured South Indian favourites on fresh banana leaf, hearty North Indian curries, Indo-Chinese delights, and a full selection of rice, biryani, snacks and beverages.
